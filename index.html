Program overview

A technology demonstrator for high-speed autonomous flight

Developed as a testbed toward a future interceptor-class variant. The current build validates the core airframe, propulsion, power and autonomous navigation subsystems, establishing the flight-proven base onto which mission sensor and guidance packages will be integrated in subsequent program phases.

Takes off nose-up, flies nose-first

A compact quadrotor in composite construction. All four wings sit at the tail, each carrying a motor with its propeller facing the nose, a layout built for high-speed flight.

For vertical take-off it lifts nose-up on its four rear motors, or it can be launched by hand. A high thrust-to-weight ratio keeps it stable in gusty conditions.

Future development roadmap

From flight demonstrator to a mission-ready variant

Capabilities planned for integration in subsequent program phases.

  1. 01

    Around 300 km/h top speed

    A propulsion and airframe revision targeting a top speed of around 300 km/h.

  2. 02

    GPS-denied navigation

    Visual-inertial odometry and optical flow.

  3. 03

    Night operations

    Low-light camera integration.

  4. 04

    Thermal imaging

    Thermal imaging payload integration.

  5. 05

    Onboard edge AI

    Real-time detection, tracking and classification on the aircraft.

  6. 06

    Swarm operations

    Coordinated multi-drone operations.

  7. 07

    Expanded payload

    Greater capacity for mission-specific sensor and effector packages.

  8. 08

    Secure datalink

    A secure, anti-jam digital datalink.

Design targets for subsequent revisions. They are not implemented on the current build.

Intended application

The technology base for the program

  1. 01Current build

    Flight demonstrator

    Validates the core airframe, propulsion, power and autonomous navigation subsystems in flight.

  2. 02Subsequent phases

    Mission sensor and guidance packages

    Integrated onto the flight-proven base as the platform matures.

  3. 03Future variant

    Interceptor-class variant

    Pending integration of target acquisition, guidance and effector subsystems in later program phases.
